# Creative brief — Pulau Laut forest scenario

## Narrative promise
A reflective, immersive short documentary asks how Pulau Laut might feel today if its natural forests had remained intact. The answer is explicitly hypothetical, not a claim about historical coverage or present ecology.

## Audience and emotional arc
Indonesian short-video audience; curiosity → wonder → ecological connection → human nuance → responsibility → invitation to care.

## Editorial rules
- Preserve the supplied voice recording and its full duration.
- Use the attached script verbatim as the subtitle source. Offline ASR weights are unavailable; word-level transcription and exact subtitle synchronization could not be independently verified.
- Label generated scenes “ILUSTRASI SKENARIO ALTERNATIF”. Never present generated scenes as Pulau Laut documentary footage or named locations/species.
- Keep the research statement general and qualified. A Kotabaru biodiversity report identifies its study location as Hutan Lindung Sebatung; do not turn its counts into island-wide claims.
- Avoid unsupported statistics, company names, fake archival treatment, and fear-based claims.
