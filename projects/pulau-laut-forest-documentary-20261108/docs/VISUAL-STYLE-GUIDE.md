# Visual style guide

- **Format:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, full 141.491 s narration (4,245 frames).
- **Direction:** premium natural-history documentary, poetic but grounded; an imagined alternative scenario, clearly labeled on screen.
- **Image language:** four generated photographic-style plates (dawn valley, canopy, stream, village edge), edited with slow camera drift, motivated crop changes, vignette, and restrained foreground parallax. Generic reconstructions, not actual Pulau Laut photographs.
- **Palette:** deep forest green and blue-green shade, warm sunrise gold, mineral river silver; natural skin and soil.
- **Composition:** broad negative space in sky or shadow for the hook; layered foreground, subject plane and distant ridges; avoid cards and repeated graphic panels. Captions stay inside vertical safe margins.
- **Type:** clean, readable sans serif; concise title and chapter phrases; captions in a dark translucent low-profile band, max two lines.
- **Motion:** slow, asymmetric camera drift with occasional push/pull and cross dissolves. No CSS-driven animation. Do not animate every object or add decorative fog/particles.
- **Factual clarity:** persistent label “ILUSTRASI SKENARIO ALTERNATIF”; research note refers to studies in specific parts of Kotabaru only.
- **Audio:** voice leads; organic, evolving original score stays low beneath speech. Effects are sparse and tied to forest/water imagery.
