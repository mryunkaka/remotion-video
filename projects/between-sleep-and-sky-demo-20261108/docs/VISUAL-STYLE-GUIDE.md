# Visual style guide

## Direction

Dreamcore as a quiet cinematic passage: familiar natural forms arranged in impossible spaces. Each shot should feel like a real place with a horizon, atmosphere, foreground, and depth. No people are used, avoiding placeholder or stiff human animation.

## Palette and light

- Night base: ink navy `#090B1D`, blue-black `#15172F`, slate violet `#4A4565`.
- Moonlight: pearl `#F4EDF0`, blue-silver `#BFC8F3`, soft mauve `#B7AFC7`.
- Dawn accent: rose `#EFB8A9`, warm ivory `#FFF0E6`.
- Keep glows restrained and localized around moon/portal; use gradients and soft bloom, with dark edges preserving contrast.

## Shape and composition

Use curved horizon lines, cloud banks, an arch, elliptical stepping forms, water ripples, and thin grass strokes. Avoid floating UI panels, card grids, rectangular scene dividers, and unnecessary labels. Keep the main title centered above the central focal object; preserve generous negative space and readable type.

## Camera and motion

Use deterministic frame-based movement only: a slow lateral drift, gentle orb float, cloud parallax, wind-driven grass sway, ascending stepping forms, breathing ripples, and a measured dawn rise. Crossfade scenes over roughly one second. Keep motion calm, with no shake, rapid zoom, or constant wobble. End on a held image with a 3-second audio fade.

## Type

Use system Georgia/serif for the expressive title and Arial for small all-caps metadata. Keep each title to two short lines, use restrained tracking, and avoid a boxed text treatment. No subtitles because there is no spoken track.

## Technical

1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 1800 frames. Every animation is a deterministic function of the Remotion frame. All artwork is authored in the production's TSX/SVG; no external downloads or reused production assets.
